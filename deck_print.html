The Solution

AroggyaGram: One Platform,
Four Critical Pillars

🤖

AI Clinical Triage

Gemini-powered multilingual symptom assessment in Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil & English. Provides risk classification, first-aid guidance, and escalation decisions — no doctor needed at Step 1.

🚨

Deterministic SOS

Keyword-triggered emergency protocols that bypass AI and directly call 9539141210 (configured rescue number) + display geo-tagged location. Falls, chest pain, unconsciousness → instant response.

💊

Medication Adherence

Smart structured med tracking with food-timing awareness, native time pickers, and daily adherence scores. Reduces the #1 cause of rural chronic illness complications — missed doses.

🤝

Volunteer Task Network

Community-verified neighbors accept geo-matched tasks for medicine pickup, clinic transport & welfare checks. AI-calculated match scores ensure the right person responds to every request.

Intelligent Orchestration

From Distress Signal to
Coordinated Response

1

Voice / Text Intake

Patient speaks or types in their native language. Natural language processing understands context, urgency, and category — not just keywords.

2

AI Situation Engine Triages

Gemini analyzes the situation against clinical heuristics. Emergency keywords → immediate SOS. Non-emergency → structured care case with risk classification (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH).

3

Volunteer Matching & Dispatch

MatchingService scores nearby verified volunteers by proximity, skills, vehicle availability, and past rating. Best match receives push notification with task details.

4

Tracked Resolution & Feedback

Unified Health Timeline records all events. Volunteer marks task complete. Patient rates experience. Data feeds back to improve future matching quality.
